# Driver portrait assets

`photos/` contains compact local copies of Wikimedia Commons photographs for every
driver who raced in the 2025 Formula 1 season. The files are resized for local UI
use; the source authors, source pages, and original licences remain below. The
Charles Leclerc asset is additionally converted from PNG to JPEG for a consistent
local image path. Each resized derivative continues under the listed source
licence. No image is presented as official Formula 1 or team artwork.

## SVG fallback badges

The SVG files directly in this folder are original, programmatic initial badges
created for this project. They contain no third-party imagery and stay available
when a local photo cannot be rendered.
